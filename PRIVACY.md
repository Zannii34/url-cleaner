# Privacy Policy — URL Cleaner

Last updated: 2026-09-12

## Summary
URL Cleaner does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

## What the Extension Does
- Reads URLs you explicitly ask it to clean (via right-click, keyboard shortcut, or popup)
- Removes tracking parameters from those URLs
- Copies the cleaned URL to your clipboard

## What the Extension Does NOT Do
- Does not collect any data
- Does not make network requests
- Does not track your browsing history
- Does not share information with third parties
- Does not use analytics

## Permissions
- **contextMenus:** For the right-click "Clean this URL" option
- **notifications:** To confirm when a URL has been cleaned
- **scripting:** To copy the cleaned URL to your clipboard
- **activeTab:** To read the current tab's URL when you press Alt+Shift+C
- **storage:** To remember your preferences
